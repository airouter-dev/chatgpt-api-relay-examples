# Prompt ideas

Use the same prompt in every language to compare SDK ergonomics:

- **Product visual:** `A matte black reusable water bottle on a pale stone plinth, soft studio light, premium ecommerce photography, no text`
- **Editorial scene:** `A tiny glass greenhouse on a rainy city rooftop at blue hour, editorial photography, realistic reflections`
- **Illustration:** `A friendly paper robot repairing a radio in a warm workshop, clean cut-paper illustration, rich but restrained colors`

For the edit step, try:

- `Turn the lighting into warm golden-hour light while preserving the composition and subject.`
- `Replace the background with a quiet alpine lake; keep the main subject unchanged.`
- `Convert the visual language to a hand-painted gouache illustration with visible brush texture.`

Avoid putting secrets, personal data, or copyrighted private material into prompts or input images unless you have permission.
